Lauren’s Production Process

  1. 01

    Discovery

Questions

Property & hospitality FAQ

Is this the same as standard real-estate photography?

No. Standard real-estate photography documents vacant space. Moments by LP builds lifestyle-driven campaign libraries that help prospects imagine living, staying, and belonging at the property—architecture, amenities, people, and South Florida atmosphere together.

Who is this for?

Luxury apartments, multifamily communities, hotels, resorts, Airbnb and vacation-rental operators, property management companies, and hospitality marketing agencies.

Can you include video and drone?

Yes. Lauren leads photography and creative direction. Video, drone, walkthroughs, and expanded production are available through Moments by LP × DAT Media and scoped to your campaign.

Do you work with marketing agencies?

Yes. Agency partners receive reliable production, clear communication, organized delivery, and assets built to campaign specifications.

How do we get started?

Book a creative discovery call. Share the property, campaign goals, timeline, and channels. Lauren will recommend the right production approach.
