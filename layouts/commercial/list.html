Usage Rights

What You Can Do With the Content

Every package includes organic commercial use. Paid advertising and full buyouts are scoped separately.

✓

Organic Commercial Use — Included

Your website, email marketing, social media (organic), Google Business, Amazon listings, Shopify, and retail displays. Use it everywhere you own the channel.

+

Paid Advertising License — Additional

If you want to use your content for paid ads, you need to purchase a paid advertising license. Running Meta ads, Google display ads, or paid placements? A license covers this. Priced by channel and duration.

✥

Full Commercial Buyout — Custom

National campaigns, retail chains, and brands that need unlimited usage across all channels and territories. Scoped and priced separately.

{{/* ══ FAQ ══ */}}

Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Which package is right for my brand?

Launching or refreshing? Start with a Brand Launch Campaign. Need consistent content every month? The Monthly Content Partnership is built for that. Product-focused? Product Photography is the fastest path. We’ll recommend the right fit on your discovery call.

What’s included in organic usage rights?

Organic commercial use covers your website, email, organic social media, Google Business, Amazon, Shopify, and retail displays anywhere you own the channel. Paid ads require a separate license.

Do you source models and props?

Yes. Lauren handles creative direction, model sourcing, and props as part of commercial production. Model and prop fees are managed for you and billed separately.

How long does a project take?

Discovery and planning typically happen within 1–2 weeks of booking. Production is scheduled based on availability. Edited galleries are delivered within 2–3 weeks after your shoot day.

Can I use the content for paid ads?

Yes, with a paid advertising license add-on. If you plan to run Meta ads, Google display, or other paid placements, we’ll scope the license by channel and duration during your discovery call.
