From Brief to Library

How Every Project Works

  1. 01

    Discovery Call

    Before anything is planned, we talk. Lauren learns about your brand, your target customer, where your content lives (Instagram, Shopify, email, ads), what’s working, what’s missing, and what the shoot needs to accomplish. This call informs everything that follows.

    30–45 minutes · Video call or phone

  2. 02

    Creative Direction & Mood Board

    Lauren builds a complete visual direction document: reference imagery, color palette, mood, styling notes, and a product-by-product shot list. You review and approve before anything is booked.

    Delivered within 3–5 business days of the discovery call

  3. 03

    Location & Logistics Planning

    Location scouting, model coordination (if applicable), prop sourcing, and scheduling. Every detail of the shoot day is planned in advance so the production day runs efficiently.

    Completed before the shoot date

  4. 04

    Production Day

    On-location shoot. Every frame is intentional. Lauren works from the approved shot list while staying flexible enough to capture unexpected moments. Product photography, lifestyle imagery, and Reel capture all happen in a single efficient production day.

    2–6 hours depending on scope

  5. 05

    Culling & Selection

    Lauren reviews the full take and selects the strongest images — ones that align with your brand direction, tell a cohesive story, and will perform across your channels. No bloated galleries of near-duplicates.

    Completed within 2–3 business days of the shoot

  6. 06

    Editing

    Professional color grading and retouching consistent with your brand aesthetic. Every image in a series looks like it belongs together. Reels are edited with music, cuts, and pacing appropriate for your platform and audience.

    7–14 business days turnaround

  7. 07

    Asset Delivery & Content Library

    Images and video delivered in a structured, organized folder system. Categorized by type (product, lifestyle, Reels), ready to upload, share with your team, or archive for future use. Monthly partners receive a cumulative library that grows each month.

    Delivered via Google Drive or similar

Why This Matters

The Difference Between a Photographer and a Production Partner

Most photographers show up, shoot, and send you a gallery. There’s no creative brief, no shot list, no organized delivery. You end up with 500 photos that are inconsistent, hard to use, and half of which you’ll never publish.

A production partner runs a process. Every project starts with a discovery call and ends with an organized content library. Between those two points, every decision — location, styling, shot composition, editing tone — is made in service of your brand and your content goals.

That’s the difference DHC saw when they invested in this work. Not “40 photos.” A complete, organized, professionally produced asset library for their product launch campaign.
