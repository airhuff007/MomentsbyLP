Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What should I wear?

Lauren helps you plan outfits during your discovery call so everything aligns with your brand. Typically 2–3 looks work well across studio and lifestyle settings.

Where do we shoot?

One hour in a professional studio, plus one hour on location somewhere that fits your brand — a coffee shop, Mizner Park, your office, or another spot we choose together.

Can I bring products?

Absolutely. Props, products, and tools that represent your business are encouraged and help tell a more complete brand story.

When will I receive my gallery?

Your edited gallery is delivered within 2–3 weeks via a private online link, ready to download and use across your website and social channels.

Can I purchase video?

Yes. Video content is available as an optional add-on, including edited reels and behind-the-scenes footage for social media.
