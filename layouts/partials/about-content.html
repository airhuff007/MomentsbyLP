My Story

More Than a Photographer

I didn’t pick up a camera because I wanted a career. I picked one up because I noticed that the moments that matter most have a way of slipping past unnoticed.

Photography is how I slow that down. Today, I help lifestyle brands show up with imagery that builds trust, tells their story, and converts on every channel that matters.

Life in Boca Raton

South Florida Is My Studio

I live in Boca Raton and I love it here. The light is different in South Florida than anywhere else I’ve been. That golden hour on the beach is something you have to experience to understand.

When I’m not shooting, you’ll find me at a local coffee shop, exploring Delray Beach, or walking Spanish River Park at sunset.

Local Favorites

  • Coffee shops with great natural light
  • Delray Beach restaurant scene
  • Spanish River Park at golden hour
  • Morikami Museum gardens
