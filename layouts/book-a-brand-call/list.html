Before You Book

Who This Call Is For

This call is for you if…

  • You represent a product-based brand in beauty, skincare, jewelry, coffee, fashion, wellness, or hospitality
  • You need consistent content monthly (photos + Reels) or a one-time launch campaign
  • Your commercial investment is $2,500+ for a one-time campaign, or $900+/month for a content partnership
  • You want to work with a creative production partner — not just a photographer
  • You’re ready to start within the next 30–60 days

This call is not for…

  • Portrait sessions, family photography, or headshots (book those here)
  • Projects under $1,000
  • Vague requests without a defined brand or goal
